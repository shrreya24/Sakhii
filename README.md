🌸 Sakhi – Women-Only Carpooling & Ride Sharing App
Sakhi is a safe, reliable, and empowering ride-sharing platform built exclusively for women. Designed to foster community and safety, Sakhi connects female commuters looking to share rides, reduce travel costs, and build trust-driven travel networks. Whether you're heading to work, college, or an event, Sakhi helps you find verified women drivers and passengers nearby.

🔒 Key Features:

👩‍👩‍👧‍👧 Women-only registration and verification

🛣️ Real-time ride discovery and matching

📍 Secure location sharing and live ride tracking

💬 In-app chat with privacy controls

🌱 Eco-conscious & cost-effective travel

Sakhi isn't just a ride—it’s a movement to reclaim the road for women, by women.
